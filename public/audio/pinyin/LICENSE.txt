Pinyin syllable recordings (FE/public/audio/pinyin/*.mp3)

Source:   mp3-chinese-pinyin-sound by davinfifield
          https://github.com/davinfifield/mp3-chinese-pinyin-sound
Commit:   aa25ecce7b7fb02757b2c2b8e3c01aa975812edc
License:  The Unlicense (public domain dedication)

The recordings were trimmed (leading/trailing silence), loudness-normalised
(ITU-R BS.1770-4, -20 LUFS, peak-limited) and re-encoded (MP3 mono 48 kbps, 32000 Hz)
by data-tools/build-pinyin-audio.mts. The file names are unchanged except:
toneless variants (de, luun, ma, zi) are dropped, zhai -> zhai4 and nue1 -> nuue1
are renamed (mislabelled), and mislabelled copies (ao4, lian4 = tone 1; de5, le5,
ma5, ne5 = copies of tone 1; dei5) are not shipped.
